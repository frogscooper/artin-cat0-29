\begin{abstract}
We construct an Artin group on 29 generators that admits no proper,
cocompact isometric action on a nonempty proper CAT(0) space.
This refutes the CAT(0) conjecture for Artin groups.
\end{abstract}

\begin{center}
\fbox{\begin{minipage}{0.92\textwidth}\small
\textbf{About this version.}
This is an edited version of the OpenAI preprint
\emph{An Artin group with no geometric CAT(0) action}
(September 23, 2026), which gives a counterexample on 116 generators.
The construction and all arguments other than the one noted below are
OpenAI's; the original is distributed under the Apache License~2.0 at
\url{https://github.com/openai/math}.

\smallskip
\textbf{What changed.}
The proof of Proposition~\ref{prop:braid-angle} is replaced by a
shorter argument that needs braid blocks of 11 strands instead of 40.
With the construction of Section~\ref{sec:assembly} otherwise
unchanged, the counterexample shrinks from 116 to 29 generators, and
the generator counts have been updated to match.

\smallskip
\textbf{Verification.}
The repository's Lean formalization of the full theorem was adapted to
the 29-generator matrix and checks it using only the axioms
\texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice} and \texttt{Quot.sound}.
This version was prepared with the assistance of Claude (Anthropic).
\end{minipage}}
